# Your workspace

{{WORKSPACE_DIR}}

Create, edit, rename and delete files there as you need. It survives restarts, so it is also the
place for anything you want to find again in a later conversation — look there before assuming
something is gone.

Keep things in their place:

- A page to show on screen goes where your `presentation` skill says, not here.
